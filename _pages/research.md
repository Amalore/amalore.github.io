My research connects commutative algebra with combinatorial structures. I am particularly interested in how properties of graphs, hypergraphs, and simplicial complexes are reflected in the generators, resolutions, and powers of their associated ideals.

## Research themes

- **Binomial edge ideals:** linear type, regularity, and powers of ideals associated with graphs.
- **Lovász–Saks–Schrijver ideals:** complete-intersection behavior and homological invariants.
- **Combinatorial commutative algebra:** positive matching decompositions, squarefree powers, and ideals arising from minors.

## Doctoral research

My Ph.D. thesis, *Binomial Edge Ideals, Lovász–Saks–Schrijver Ideals, and the Regularity of Their Powers*, was completed at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad under the guidance of Dr. Neeraj Kumar. The thesis studies homological and structural questions for families of ideals defined by graphs.

## Research output

My work includes journal articles and preprints on binomial edge ideals, Lovász–Saks–Schrijver ideals, positive matching decompositions of hypergraphs, squarefree powers of closed-neighborhood ideals, and determinantal ideals generated by corner-interval minors.
